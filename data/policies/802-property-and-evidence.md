# Policy 802 — Property and Evidence

> Kestrel County Sheriff's Office Policy Manual · Effective 2023-03-01 · Revised 2025-09-15
> FICTIONAL POLICY FOR DEMONSTRATION PURPOSES ONLY

## 802.1 Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes procedures for the collection, packaging, documentation, storage, and disposal of property and evidence that comes into the custody of the Kestrel County Sheriff's Office. Properly handled evidence is essential to successful prosecutions and to protecting the rights of the accused. Properly handled found and safekeeping property protects the rights of owners and the integrity of the Office.

## 802.2 Definitions
**Evidence** — Any item or data seized or collected that may be used to prove or disprove a fact in a criminal or administrative matter.

**Chain of custody** — The documented, unbroken record of every person who has had custody of an item of evidence, from collection to final disposition.

**Found property** — Property found by or turned in to a member that has no apparent connection to a crime.

**Safekeeping property** — Property taken into custody to protect it for its owner, such as property of an arrested or hospitalized person.

**Digital evidence** — Information of investigative value that is stored or transmitted in digital form, including video, photographs, phone extractions, and computer data.

**Temporary evidence locker** — A secured locker at a district station where members deposit packaged items until property technicians move them to the central property room.

## 802.3 Collection and Chain of Custody
The member who collects an item is responsible for it until it is secured in a temporary evidence locker or transferred to a property technician. Members shall document where each item was found, the time of collection, and the name of the collector. Every transfer of an item between persons shall be recorded on the chain of custody record with the date, time, names, and purpose of the transfer.

Members shall handle evidence only as needed and use protective gloves to avoid contamination. Members shall not keep evidence in personal lockers, vehicles, or desks, and shall not take property for personal use.

## 802.4 Packaging and Labeling
Each item shall be packaged separately when practical, in a container appropriate to its type, and sealed with evidence tape bearing the packaging member's initials and the date. Each package shall be labeled with the case number, item number, description, and the collector's name. Specific requirements include:

- **Biological evidence** — Air-dried and packaged in paper, never plastic; refrigerated when required.
- **Firearms** — Unloaded, rendered safe, and packaged in a firearm box with ammunition packaged separately. Serial numbers shall be checked through the appropriate databases.
- **Narcotics** — Weighed, field-tested when safe, and sealed in heat-sealed bags with the gross weight noted. Suspected fentanyl shall not be field-tested and shall be packaged with additional precautions.
- **Currency** — Counted by two members, with both signing the package.
- **Sharps** — Placed in puncture-resistant tubes and labeled as hazardous.

## 802.5 Submission Timelines
All property and evidence shall be entered into the property management system and placed in a temporary evidence locker before the end of the member's shift. Perishable items, large items, and hazardous materials shall be handled as directed by the property room supervisor. The property entry shall match the report and face sheet descriptions.

## 802.6 Digital Evidence
Digital evidence shall be preserved in its original form and uploaded to the digital evidence management system before the end of shift. Members shall not alter, edit, or delete original digital files. Photographs and recordings from personal devices are prohibited except in exigent circumstances, in which case the files shall be uploaded and then deleted from the personal device as directed by a supervisor.

Seized phones and computers shall be secured to prevent remote wiping, such as by placing them in airplane mode or a signal-blocking bag, and shall be searched only under a warrant or valid exception as described in Policy 322. Forensic extractions shall be performed by trained examiners using validated tools, and a hash value shall be recorded to verify the integrity of each extraction.

## 802.7 Property Room Security
Access to the central property room is limited to assigned property technicians and the property room supervisor. All other persons, including investigators, shall be escorted and logged. The property room shall have intrusion alarms and video surveillance, and access logs shall be retained. High-value items, firearms, narcotics, and currency shall be stored in separate secured areas.

## 802.8 Release of Evidence for Court or Lab
Members checking out evidence for court, laboratory analysis, or examination shall sign the chain of custody record and return the item by the end of shift unless it is retained by the court or lab. Any opening of a sealed package shall be documented, and the package shall be resealed with new tape and initials.

## 802.9 Audits and Inventories
The property room supervisor shall conduct an annual inventory of high-value items, firearms, narcotics, and currency. An unannounced audit shall be conducted by a member appointed by the Sheriff at least once each year and whenever the property room supervisor changes. Discrepancies shall be reported immediately to the Professional Standards Unit.

## 802.10 Disposition and Disposal
Evidence shall be retained until the case is resolved and disposal is authorized by the investigator, prosecutor, or court order, as required by applicable state law. Biological evidence in violent felony cases shall be retained for the period required by law. Found and safekeeping property shall be returned to the owner or disposed of after the holding period required by applicable state law. Firearms and narcotics shall be destroyed in the presence of two witnesses, and the destruction documented.
