# Policy 1030 — Inmate Release Procedures

> Kestrel County Sheriff's Office Policy Manual · Effective 2024-03-01 · Revised 2026-06-01
> FICTIONAL POLICY FOR DEMONSTRATION PURPOSES ONLY

## 1030.1 Purpose and Scope
Releasing an inmate from the Kestrel County Detention Center is a critical function. An erroneous release can endanger victims and the public, while holding an inmate beyond the lawful release time violates the inmate's rights. This policy establishes procedures for verifying release authority, checking for holds and detainers, confirming identity, notifying victims, and processing inmates out of the Detention Center safely and on time.

## 1030.2 Definitions
**Release authority** — A court order, bond posting, sentence completion, charge dismissal, citation release, or other lawful basis for releasing an inmate.

**Hold** — A documented reason to keep an inmate in custody, such as a warrant from another jurisdiction, a parole or probation hold, or a court order.

**Detainer** — A request from another agency, including federal agencies, that the Detention Center notify it before an inmate's release or hold the inmate, which is honored only as permitted by law.

**Erroneous release** — The release of an inmate who should have remained in custody, or of the wrong person.

**Release officer** — The detention deputy assigned to process a release.

## 1030.3 Release Date Verification
Before any release, Records staff shall verify the release authority and calculate the release date and time. For sentenced inmates, the calculation shall account for credit for time served, good-time credit, and consecutive or concurrent sentences, as provided by applicable state law and the sentencing order. A second Records staff member shall independently verify every sentence calculation before the release date is entered in the jail management system.

When the release authority is unclear or appears inconsistent, Records staff shall contact the court clerk or issuing agency before release and document the clarification.

## 1030.4 Holds and Detainers Check
Before release, Records staff shall conduct a final check of all applicable systems for warrants, holds, and detainers, including the state and national criminal justice information systems, using the inmate's full name, aliases, date of birth, and identification numbers. The check shall be completed no more than two hours before the release and documented on the release checklist.

When a valid warrant or hold is found, the inmate shall not be released and the requesting agency shall be notified. Detainers that do not include a judicial warrant or order shall be handled according to applicable state and federal law and the Office's procedures, and the Records Supervisor shall be consulted when there is any question.

## 1030.5 Identity Verification
To prevent the release of the wrong person, the release officer shall confirm the inmate's identity using at least two independent methods before release, one of which shall be electronic fingerprint verification. Acceptable methods include:

- Comparing the inmate's face to the booking photograph.
- Confirming the inmate's name, date of birth, and booking number by asking the inmate to state them.
- Comparing the wristband to the jail management system record.
- Electronic fingerprint verification against the booking record.

Fingerprint verification is required for every release. When there is any discrepancy, the release shall be stopped and a supervisor notified.

## 1030.6 Victim Notification
For inmates charged with or convicted of offenses involving a victim, Records staff shall ensure that registered victims are notified of the release through the automated victim notification system and, when required by applicable state law or court order, by direct contact. Notification of domestic violence, stalking, and sexual assault victims shall occur before the release whenever possible. Staff shall document the notification and any attempt that failed. Release shall not be delayed beyond the lawful release time solely because a victim could not be reached, but a supervisor shall be notified so additional efforts can be made.

## 1030.7 Property and Release Processing
The release officer shall return the inmate's personal property and funds according to the property inventory, obtain the inmate's signature, and document any discrepancies. Inmates shall be provided with any medications or prescriptions as directed by medical staff, and with information on community resources, including mental-health and substance-treatment services. Inmates released during overnight hours shall be offered the opportunity to remain in the lobby or contact transportation.

## 1030.8 Supervisor Approval
A detention supervisor shall review and approve the release checklist before the inmate exits the secure perimeter. The checklist shall confirm the release authority, sentence calculation verification, holds check, identity verification, victim notification, and property return.

## 1030.9 Erroneous Release and Over-Detention
If an erroneous release is discovered, staff shall immediately notify the shift supervisor, who shall notify the Detention Center Commander, enter appropriate notices, and request patrol assistance to locate the person. Any victim shall be notified immediately. If an inmate is found to have been held beyond the lawful release time, the inmate shall be released immediately after required checks and the incident reported to the Detention Center Commander. Both types of incidents shall be reviewed to identify the cause and corrective action.

## 1030.10 Audits
The Records Supervisor shall audit a random sample of releases each month for compliance with this policy and report findings to the Detention Center Commander.
