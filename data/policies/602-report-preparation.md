# Policy 602 — Report Preparation

> Kestrel County Sheriff's Office Policy Manual · Effective 2024-05-01 · Revised 2026-07-01
> FICTIONAL POLICY FOR DEMONSTRATION PURPOSES ONLY

## 602.1 Purpose and Scope
Written reports are the foundation of criminal prosecutions, civil proceedings, and the Office's institutional record. Prosecutors, courts, defense attorneys, supervisors, and the public rely on them to understand what happened and why deputies acted. This policy establishes when reports are required and the standards for timeliness, content, accuracy, and approval of reports prepared by members of the Kestrel County Sheriff's Office.

## 602.2 Definitions
**Face sheet** — The structured data portion of a report in the records management system, including the incident type, date, time, location, involved persons, vehicles, and property.

**Narrative** — The written account of the incident prepared by the reporting member.

**Supplemental report** — A report that adds information to an existing case, completed by the original deputy or another member.

**AI-assisted drafting** — The use of an Office-approved generative AI or decision-support tool, governed by Policy 830, to help prepare any part of a report.

## 602.3 Required Reports
Members shall complete a report for:

- Any criminal offense reported to or discovered by the member.
- Any arrest, citation in lieu of arrest, or detention involving force, handcuffing, or transport.
- Any use of force, control device deployment, or vehicle pursuit.
- Domestic violence calls, regardless of whether a crime is found, under Policy 320.
- Property and evidence seized or found.
- Traffic collisions as required by Office procedures.
- Any other incident a supervisor directs to be documented.

## 602.4 Timeliness
Reports shall be completed and submitted before the end of the member's shift. A supervisor may approve a delay for a report that does not involve an in-custody arrest when exceptional circumstances exist, such as the member's injury or a lengthy investigation late in the shift. Approved delayed reports shall be completed by the end of the member's next shift. Reports involving an arrest, use of force, or a vulnerable victim shall not be delayed except with watch commander approval.

## 602.5 Required Narrative Elements
The narrative shall give a complete, chronological account that allows a reader with no knowledge of the incident to understand it. It shall include, as applicable:

- **Who** — All involved persons, their roles, and how they were identified.
- **What** — What happened, what each person did and said, and what the member observed directly.
- **When and where** — Dates, times, and specific locations.
- **How and why** — How the incident occurred and the basis for each enforcement action.
- **Elements of the offense** — Facts establishing each element of every offense charged.
- **Miranda** — Whether warnings were given, by whom, at what time, and whether the person waived or invoked rights.
- **Evidence** — Items collected, by whom, where found, and their disposition.
- **Witnesses** — Names, contact information, and a summary of each statement.
- **Recordings** — Whether body-worn camera footage exists and whether it was reviewed.

## 602.6 Objective and Factual Language
Reports shall describe what the member observed, heard, and did, using specific and objective language. Members shall avoid unsupported conclusions and vague phrases. For example, rather than writing that a person "acted suspicious," "was combative," or "appeared nervous," the member shall describe the observed behavior, such as "looked repeatedly toward the open driver's door while his right hand stayed inside his jacket pocket."

Opinions and inferences are permitted only when supported by the facts described and identified as the member's belief. Quotations should be used for significant statements. Members shall not include personal commentary, slang, or derogatory language.

## 602.7 Accuracy and Consistency
Members are responsible for the accuracy of every report they author. The face sheet and the narrative shall be consistent, including names, dates of birth, addresses, times, charges, and property descriptions. Members shall correct any discrepancy before submitting the report.

Knowingly making a false or misleading statement, or intentionally omitting material information from a report, is serious misconduct that may result in termination and criminal prosecution. Errors discovered after approval shall be corrected through a supplemental report rather than by altering the original.

## 602.8 AI-Assisted Drafting
Members may use AI-assisted drafting only through tools approved under Policy 830 (Generative AI and Decision-Support Tools) and only after completing the required training. AI-assisted drafting is a convenience and does not change the member's responsibility for the report.

The deputy is the author of the report and attests to its accuracy. Before submitting, the deputy shall read the entire draft, verify every fact against the deputy's own knowledge, recordings, and source records, and remove or correct any content that is inaccurate, speculative, or not personally known. Any report prepared with AI assistance shall include the disclosure statement required by Policy 830. AI tools shall not be used to generate the deputy's description of their own perceptions or reasons for using force.

## 602.9 Supervisor Review and Approval
Supervisors shall review reports for completeness, accuracy, legal sufficiency, objective language, and compliance with policy, and shall approve or return them with specific corrections before the end of the supervisor's shift whenever practical. Supervisors shall verify that reports prepared with AI assistance include the required disclosure. Approved reports shall not be changed except through the supplemental process.

## 602.10 Report Corrections and Retention
Returned reports shall be corrected and resubmitted by the member's next shift. All versions, rejection notes, and approvals are retained in the records management system according to the Office retention schedule.
