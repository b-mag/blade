# Policy 810 — Records Release and Public Records Requests

> Kestrel County Sheriff's Office Policy Manual · Effective 2024-09-01 · Revised 2026-08-01
> FICTIONAL POLICY FOR DEMONSTRATION PURPOSES ONLY

## 810.1 Purpose and Scope
The Kestrel County Sheriff's Office is committed to transparency and to providing public access to its records as required by applicable state law. At the same time, the Office has a duty to protect the privacy and safety of victims, witnesses, juveniles, and others, and to protect ongoing investigations and criminal justice information. This policy establishes how requests for Office records are received, reviewed, redacted, and released.

## 810.2 Definitions
**Public record** — Any record prepared, owned, used, or retained by the Office in the course of its business, in any format, as defined by applicable state law.

**Records Custodian** — The Records Supervisor, or designee, responsible for responding to records requests on behalf of the Office.

**Redaction** — The removal or obscuring of information that is exempt from disclosure before a record is released.

**Criminal justice information (CJI)** — Data provided by FBI CJIS systems and related state systems, including criminal history records, as described in Policy 820.

## 810.3 Receiving Requests
Records requests may be submitted in person, by mail, by email, or through the Office's online request portal. Members who receive a request shall forward it to the Records Unit on the same day. Requesters are not required to explain the purpose of their request unless applicable state law requires it for certain record types. The Records Unit shall log each request with the date received, the records sought, and the response deadline.

## 810.4 Response Timelines
The Records Custodian shall acknowledge and respond to each request within the time required by applicable state law. When additional time is needed because of the volume of records, the need to search multiple locations, or the need for review by an investigator or legal counsel, the Records Custodian shall notify the requester in writing of the reason and the expected response date, within the limits permitted by law. Requests shall not be delayed to discourage the requester.

## 810.5 Review Before Release
Before any record is released, the Records Unit shall review it in full. When a record relates to an open investigation, the assigned investigator or the Investigations Division supervisor shall be consulted to determine whether release would interfere with the investigation, endanger a person, or reveal confidential sources or techniques. Body-worn camera and other video recordings shall be reviewed in their entirety before release, consistent with Policy 342. Records that may be subject to litigation or that involve a critical incident shall be reviewed by County Counsel.

## 810.6 Required Redactions
Unless release is required by law or court order, the following information shall be redacted from released records:

- Names, addresses, phone numbers, and other identifying information of victims of sexual offenses, domestic violence, stalking, and human trafficking.
- Information identifying juveniles, consistent with Policy 324.
- Criminal justice information obtained from CJIS systems, including criminal history records, as required by Policy 820.
- Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, financial account numbers, and dates of birth when not otherwise public.
- Medical, mental-health, and substance-treatment information.
- Identities of confidential informants and undercover members.
- Home addresses and personal contact information of members.
- Information whose release is otherwise prohibited by applicable state or federal law.

Each redaction shall be marked so the requester can see that information was withheld, and the legal basis shall be noted in the response.

## 810.7 Denials
When all or part of a record is withheld, the Records Custodian shall provide a written response that identifies the records withheld, the specific legal exemption relied upon, and the process for appealing or seeking review, as provided by applicable state law. Denials of records relating to critical incidents shall be reviewed by the Undersheriff before being issued.

## 810.8 Fees
Fees for copying, media, and staff time shall be charged only as allowed by applicable state law and the County fee schedule. The Records Custodian may waive fees for victims requesting their own reports, for requests in the public interest, or as otherwise authorized.

## 810.9 Subpoenas and Court Orders
Subpoenas duces tecum and court orders for records shall be forwarded immediately to the Records Unit, which shall coordinate with County Counsel when the subpoena seeks confidential records or member personnel files. Personnel records are confidential and shall be released only as required by law or court order.

## 810.10 Media and Critical Incident Releases
Information released to the media is governed by the Office's media relations procedures. In critical incidents, the Sheriff may authorize the proactive release of records, including video, to inform the public, after consultation with the prosecuting attorney and County Counsel and after applying the redactions required by this policy.

## 810.11 Unauthorized Release
Members shall not release Office records, photographs, recordings, or information outside of the procedures in this policy. Unauthorized release of confidential records or CJI may result in discipline and criminal penalties.
