# Policy 1010 — Inmate Classification and Housing

> Kestrel County Sheriff's Office Policy Manual · Effective 2023-05-15 · Revised 2025-04-10
> FICTIONAL POLICY FOR DEMONSTRATION PURPOSES ONLY

## 1010.1 Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes the classification system used to assign inmates to housing at the Kestrel County Detention Center. Objective classification protects inmates, staff, and visitors by placing each inmate in the least restrictive housing consistent with safety and security. It also ensures that inmates with medical, mental-health, or protective needs are identified and housed appropriately. This policy applies to all detention deputies, classification staff, and supervisors at the Detention Center.

## 1010.2 Definitions
**Classification** — The process of evaluating an inmate's risks and needs to determine custody level and housing assignment.

**Custody level** — The level of supervision and security an inmate requires: minimum, medium, or maximum.

**Keep-separate order** — An instruction in the jail management system that two or more inmates must not be housed together or have contact with one another.

**Protective custody** — Housing that separates an inmate from the general population for the inmate's own safety.

**Administrative segregation** — Non-disciplinary separation of an inmate from the general population because the inmate presents a serious threat to safety or security.

## 1010.3 Initial Classification
Every inmate shall be screened at booking for immediate safety concerns, including medical needs, suicide risk, intoxication, signs of victimization, and known enemies. Inmates shall be held in intake housing until a classification interview is completed, which shall occur within 24 hours of booking whenever practical.

Classification staff shall use the Office's objective classification instrument, which considers the current charges, prior convictions, history of institutional violence or escape, pending holds, gang affiliation, age, and stability factors. Classification decisions shall not be based on race, ethnicity, religion, or national origin.

## 1010.4 Custody Levels
- **Minimum** — Inmates charged with non-violent offenses, with no history of institutional violence or escape. Eligible for dormitory housing and work programs.
- **Medium** — Inmates charged with offenses involving violence or with moderate institutional history. Housed in general population cell units.
- **Maximum** — Inmates charged with serious violent felonies, with a history of escape or serious assault on staff or inmates, or who present a significant threat to security. Housed in single cells with restricted movement and escort requirements.

Classification staff may override the instrument's score by one level with supervisor approval and a documented reason.

## 1010.5 Keep-Separate Orders
Keep-separate orders shall be entered in the jail management system for:

- Co-defendants, unless the prosecuting attorney or court indicates separation is unnecessary.
- Inmates who have been involved in a fight, assault, or threat against one another.
- Rival gang members or members of security threat groups known to be in conflict.
- Victims and suspects in the same case.
- Inmates identified as enemies by the inmate, staff, or outside agencies.

Before any housing move, court movement, recreation, or program assignment, staff shall check for keep-separate conflicts. Keep-separate orders shall be removed only with classification supervisor approval.

## 1010.6 Gang and Security Threat Group Considerations
Classification staff shall review available information about gang affiliation and security threat group membership, including self-reports, tattoos, and intelligence from other agencies. Affiliation information shall be used to prevent conflicts and manage risk and shall be documented with its source. Affiliation alone shall not result in maximum custody without other risk factors.

## 1010.7 Protective Custody
An inmate may be placed in protective custody upon request, upon staff recommendation, or when information indicates the inmate faces a substantial risk of harm. Inmates who may require protection include those who are former law enforcement officers, witnesses, high-profile defendants, or persons charged with offenses that may make them targets. Inmates at risk of sexual victimization shall be identified and housed in accordance with applicable federal standards for the prevention of sexual abuse in confinement.

Inmates in protective custody shall receive access to programs, recreation, and services comparable to the general population whenever safely possible.

## 1010.8 Medical and Mental-Health Flags
Medical and mental-health staff shall enter housing flags in the jail management system for inmates with conditions that affect housing, such as communicable diseases, mobility impairments, pregnancy, withdrawal risk, or serious mental illness. Flags indicate housing needs without disclosing diagnoses to non-medical staff. Inmates identified as a suicide risk shall be housed in observation housing and monitored as directed by mental-health staff. Classification staff shall not house an inmate in a way that conflicts with a medical or mental-health flag.

## 1010.9 Reclassification
Inmates shall be reclassified:

- At least every 60 days for inmates in general population.
- At least every 30 days for inmates in maximum custody, administrative segregation, or protective custody.
- Within 72 hours after a significant change, such as new charges, a sentence, a disciplinary finding, or new information about enemies or medical needs.

Inmates in administrative segregation shall be reviewed by a supervisor at least every seven days to determine whether continued separation is necessary.

## 1010.10 Documentation and Appeals
All classification decisions, overrides, and housing moves shall be documented in the jail management system with the reason and the name of the deciding staff member. Inmates may appeal a classification decision through the inmate grievance process, and the classification supervisor shall respond in writing.
